The chainsaw was originally invented by two Scottish doctors in the late 18th century to assist with difficult childbirths. Before Cesarean sections were viable, physicians used a hand-cranked tool with a chain of teeth to rapidly cut through a woman's pelvic bone to make room for the baby to pass. It wasn't until over a century later that people realized the gruesome surgical instrument was also highly effective at cutting down trees.
