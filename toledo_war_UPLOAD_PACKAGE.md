## Description

```
In 1835, Ohio and Michigan almost went to war.

Not over slavery. Not over taxes.

Over a strip of marshland called the Toledo Strip. About 468 square miles.

Both states claimed it. Ohio had political power in Congress. Michigan was just a territory wanting statehood.

The governor of Ohio called up the militia. Michigan's governor called up his. They sent armed men to the border.

For months, they camped in the snow, eyeing each other across the line. Both sides arrested each other's officials. There were fistfights. A sheriff got stabbed.

The only actual battle? A Michigan militia fired a volley of snowballs at Ohio scouts.

Congress had to intervene. Ohio got the Toledo Strip. Michigan got the Upper Peninsula -- which turned out to be loaded with copper and iron ore.

So Michigan arguably won the peace.

The Toledo War: America's dumbest almost-war. Zero deaths, one stabbing, a lot of yelling.

Follow for more things they didn't teach you in school.
```
