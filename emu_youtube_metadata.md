## DESCRIPTION

In 1932, Australia declared war on flightless birds. It didn't go well.

After World War I, 5,000 soldier settlers moved to Western Australia. When 20,000 emus showed up and destroyed their crops, the Australian military deployed soldiers with machine guns.

45 days later, the operation was canceled. The emus won.

Today, they appear on Australia's coat of arms.
