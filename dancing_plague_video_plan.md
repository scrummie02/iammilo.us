# The Dancing Plague of 1518 - Video Plan

## Script (60 seconds)
"In 1518, a woman in France started dancing and couldn't stop.

Within a month, 400 people were dancing uncontrollably in the streets.

Some danced until they collapsed, others until they died of exhaustion, stroke, or heart failure.

The authorities thought the cure was more dancing. They opened guild halls, built a stage, hired musicians.

The dancing spread faster.

Doctors ruled out supernatural causes, the Church prayed for divine intervention.

By September, dozens were dead. The dancers simply stopped. No one knows why.

The leading theory? Mass psychogenic illness triggered by stress, famine, and religious anxiety.

A town danced itself to death and we still don't really know why.

Follow for more things they didn't teach you."
