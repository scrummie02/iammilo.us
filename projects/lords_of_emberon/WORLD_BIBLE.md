# Lords of Emberon - World Bible

## 🌎 The Universe
**Emberon:** A world forged in dragon-fire and sustained by the "Great Ember"—a primordial flame at the planet's core. Magic is volatile, tied to the heat and ash of the land.

---

## 🏛️ The Great Hegemonies (Starting Nations)

### 1. The Valerian Empire (Human)
*   **Vibe:** High-Fantasy Roman/Holy Roman Empire. Strict hierarchy, massive stone fortresses, and disciplined legions.
*   **The Ember Connection:** They use "Purge-Fire" to fuel their industrial war-machine and gunpowder weapons.
*   **Perk:** +1 to Charisma (Command) and Melee Defense.

### 2. The Kingdom of Lyoness (Human)
*   **Vibe:** Arthurian Legend / Bretonnia. A land of emerald hills, mist-shrouded lakes, and towering castles. Highly focused on chivalry, heraldry, and heavy cavalry (Grail Knights).
*   **The Ember Connection:** They believe in the "Lady of the Flame"—a mystical entity who grants holy fire to their blades.
*   **Perk:** +1 to Strength and Mounted Combat / Courage.

### 3. The Solari Sultanate (Human/Mystic)
*   **Vibe:** Golden deserts, vast oasis-cities, and astral observatories. 
*   **The Ember Connection:** Sun-worshipers who channel the heat of the desert into powerful beam-based magic and alchemy.
*   **Perk:** +1 to Intellect (Arcane) and Heat Resistance.

### 4. The Aethelgard Dominion (Elven)
*   **Vibe:** Ancient "Ever-Burning" forests. Trees with silver bark and leaves made of cooling embers. 
*   **The Ember Connection:** They are the guardians of the oldest dragon-shrines. Their magic is graceful, flowing like liquid fire.
*   **Perk:** +1 to Agility and Animal Handling (Dragons/Beasts).

### 5. The Khazad-Kar Hegemony (Dwarven)
*   **Vibe:** Cities carved into volcanic roots. Vast geothermal-powered forges and obsidian architecture.
*   **The Ember Connection:** Masters of "Core-Forging"—shaping items from the living magma of Emberon.
*   **Perk:** +1 to Strength and Crafting (Smithing).

### 6. The Iron-Vale Free-States (Human/Independent)
*   **Vibe:** Gritty dragon-hunter clans living in the cold mountain passes. Independent and wary of the Empire.
*   **The Ember Connection:** They use dragon-blood rituals to grant themselves unnatural toughness.
*   **Perk:** +1 to Endurance and Survival.

### 7. The Glimmer-Coast Trade-League (Diverse)
*   **Vibe:** Merchant-Princes, pirates, and smugglers. Cities built on obsidian-sand piers and ship-wrecks.
*   **The Ember Connection:** They control the trade of Ember-Shards (the world's magical fuel/currency).
*   **Perk:** +1 to Bartering and Perception.

### 8. The Verdant Conclave (Druids/Rangers)
*   **Vibe:** A secretive confederation of druids, rangers, and hedge-witches living in the deep forests between the other nations. No king — just elders.
*   **The Ember Connection:** They believe the Great Ember is a *living creature* that must be healed, not fed or extinguished. They are the only ones actively studying what's wrong with it.
*   **Perk:** +1 to Nature Magic and Herbalism/Poison.

## 📖 Creation Myth / Opening Backstory

*In the age before memory, there was only the Void — and within it, a single heartbeat.*

*The First Dragon, known only as **Embron the Undying**, exhaled.*

*That breath became the world.*

*His fire forged the mountains. His blood filled the seas with obsidian tides. His scales, shed across millennia, became the **Ember-Shards** — fragments of pure creation scattered across the land. And when Embron finally laid down to sleep at the world's core, his body became the **Great Ember** — the volcanic heart that keeps Emberon alive to this day.*

*For a thousand years, the Eight Hegemonies of Emberon lived in uneasy peace, each nation carving their civilization from the dragon's bones.*

*But something has changed.*

*The Great Ember... is fading.*

*The tremors started three seasons ago. The Ember-Shards are dimming. The dragons — once sleeping in their mountain lairs — are waking, angry, and confused. And in the shadows, a cult whispers that the only way to relight the world's heart... is to feed it.*

*You are a wanderer from one of the Eight Hegemonies. You don't know it yet — but you are the only one who can find out what's really happening to Emberon.*

*Your story begins at a tavern. It always begins at a tavern.*

## ⚔️ Player Classes

### 1. Vanguard (The Fighter)
*   **Role:** Frontline tank and melee specialist.
*   **Emberon Twist:** Their armor is Core-Forged (Dwarven volcanic steel). They can perform "Ember-Strikes" — flame-infused attacks that deal bonus fire damage.
*   **Best Nation Pairing:** Valerian Empire, Iron-Vale Free-States, Kingdom of Lyoness.

### 2. Arcanist (The Wizard)
*   **Role:** High-damage magic dealer but fragile.
*   **Emberon Twist:** They weave volatile Ember-magic. Spells can "overheat" and explode for massive damage — or backfire. High risk, high reward.
*   **Best Nation Pairing:** Aethelgard Dominion, Solari Sultanate.

### 3. Infiltrator (The Rogue)
*   **Role:** Stealth, critical hits, and trap-setting.
*   **Emberon Twist:** They use Ember-Shards as weapons — throwing them to create flash-bangs, smoke screens, or small explosions.
*   **Best Nation Pairing:** Glimmer-Coast Trade-League, Iron-Vale Free-States.

### 4. Templar (The Cleric)
*   **Role:** Healer and holy warrior hybrid.
*   **Emberon Twist:** They channel the Light of the Great Ember. Can "Purge" cursed wounds, undead, or Ash-Bound cultists with sacred fire.
*   **Best Nation Pairing:** Kingdom of Lyoness, Valerian Empire.

### 5. Ash-Singer (The Bard)
*   **Role:** Support, diplomacy, and crowd control.
*   **Emberon Twist:** Their music resonates with the ancient dragon-frequencies buried in Emberon's core. They can stabilize volatile magic, buff allies, blind enemies with Ash-Clouds, and unlock unique lore-based dialogue options that no other class can access.
*   **Special Ability — Echoes of the Past:** Can "read" ruins and artifacts for hidden information.
*   **Best Nation Pairing:** Aethelgard Dominion, Solari Sultanate, Glimmer-Coast Trade-League.

## 🎭 Factions & Cults (NPCs / Quests)

### The Ash-Bound Hegemony (The Cult)
*   **Vibe:** A fanatical, underground cult that has hijacked the name of an old nation.
*   **Lore:** They worship the "Primal Drakes" and believe that Emberon should be returned to the dragons. They operate in the shadows of the Valerian Empire and Lyoness, trying to subvert the Great Hegemonies.
*   **Role:** Primary antagonist faction for the starter campaign.
