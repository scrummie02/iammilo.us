## Key Facts
- **Scientific Name:** Turritopsis dohrnii
- **Size:** ~4.5mm (size of pinky nail)
- **Discovered:** 1988
- **Habitat:** Mediterranean Sea, Japanese waters, now spreading globally
- **Superpower:** Transdifferentiation - cells transform from one type to another
- **Process:** Adult medusa → polyp (baby) → adult medusa (infinite loop)
- **Research Applications:** Cancer research, aging studies, regenerative medicine
- **Limit:** No known limit to cycles (theoretically immortal)
- **Caveat:** Can still die from disease, predators, physical destruction
