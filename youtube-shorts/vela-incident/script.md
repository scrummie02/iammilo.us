# Vela Incident - YouTube Short Script
## Topic: The Nuclear Flash No One Claimed

---

### HOOK (0:00-0:03)
September 22, 1979. A Cold War satellite saw something nobody could explain.

---

### BODY (0:03-0:45)

An American Vela satellite — designed to catch nuclear tests — detected a "double flash" over the South Atlantic.

That's the signature of a nuclear explosion.

Estimated yield: 2 to 3 kilotons. Same as Hiroshima.

US scientists were convinced. Even President Carter wrote in his diary: "We have a growing belief that the Israelis did indeed conduct a nuclear test."

But here's where it gets weird.

No country claimed it.

The official story? It was "inconclusive" — maybe a meteor, maybe a glitch, maybe nothing at all.

But sheep in Australia suddenly had radioactive iodine in their thyroids.

And decades later, declassified documents revealed the CIA believed it was real.

The leading theory? A secret joint Israeli-South African nuclear test.

Both had the motive. Both had the means. Neither had the courage to admit it.

---

### CTA (0:45-0:60)
A nuclear explosion in the ocean — covered up for decades. And we still don't have the full truth.

Follow for more things they didn't teach you in school.
