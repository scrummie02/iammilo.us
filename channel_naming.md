# Channel Name Ideas - Obscure History & Science

## Current Working Name
**"What You Didn't Learn in School"**
- Pro: Clear value proposition
- Con: A bit long, generic

## Alternative Names

### History-Focused
1. **Classroom Lies** — implies the untold truth
2. **The Footnotes** — what they left out of textbooks
3. **After the Bell** — what happens after history class
4. **The Margin Notes** — the scribbles they erased
5. **Omitted** — simple, direct

### Science-Focused
6. **Lab Notes** — behind the experiments
7. **The Control Group** — what they didn't test
8. **Unpublished Results** — the failed experiments
9. **Peer Review** — fact-checking everything

### Mixed History + Science
10. **The Appendix** — extra material they cut
11. **Extra Credit** — bonus knowledge
12. **Study Hall** — learning outside class
13. **The Syllabus** — what's not on the test
14. **Detention Facts** — the punishment you wanted
15. **Pop Quiz** — quick hits of weird knowledge

### Tone-Based
16. **Wait, What?** — reaction-based, very clickable
17. **Plot Holes** — history's inconsistencies
18. **The Asterisk** — the caveat they ignored
19. **Fine Print** — the details they skimmed
20. **Actually...** — correcting what you learned

### My Top 3
1. **The Footnotes** — elegant, implies depth
2. **After the Bell** — relatable, school imagery
3. **Wait, What?** — highest click potential

## Logo Concepts

### Concept 1: The Footnotes
- Visual: Open book with tiny superscript numbers floating out
- Color: Deep navy + gold (academic but premium)
- Font: Serif for "The" sans-serif for "Footnotes"

### Concept 2: After the Bell
- Visual: School bell ringing with facts bursting out
- Color: Retro schoolhouse red + chalkboard black
- Font: Bold, slightly distressed

### Concept 3: Wait, What?
- Visual: Person with hands on head in disbelief
- Color: High contrast, neon accent on "What?"
- Font: Impact-style, immediate readability

### Concept 4: The Appendix
- Visual: Scroll unfurling with weird symbols and diagrams
- Color: Aged parchment + deep red ink
- Font: Typewriter meets modern sans

### Concept 5: Omitted
- Visual: Redacted document with text bleeding through
- Color: Black, white, with redaction bars
- Font: Clean monospace like a typewriter
